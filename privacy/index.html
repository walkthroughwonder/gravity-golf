Gravity Golf Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

The guest mobile edition of Gravity Golf does not collect, sell, or transmit personal information. It does not require an account and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Local game data

Progress, settings, records, and unlock state are stored locally on the device. Deleting the app removes this locally stored data.

Network access

Core gameplay, fonts, graphics, code, and music are included with the app. The guest edition does not need network access to play.

Questions

Policy updates and project contact information are available at gravitygolf.xyz.
