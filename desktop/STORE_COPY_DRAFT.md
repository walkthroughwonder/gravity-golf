## Short Description

Bend your shots around planets in a cosmic game of mini-golf. Learn the controls
in an interactive flight lesson, tackle 19 authored holes, and chase a cleaner
line through the gravity wells.

## About This Game

The fairway is an orbit.

Pick your ball, line up a shot, and let planetary gravity shape its path.
Gravity Golf turns mini-golf into a journey between small worlds, deep-space
obstacles and black-hole hazards. A shot that misses a straight line can still
find its way home.

Start in the interactive Launch Lab, then explore the full 19-hole course.
Replay a favorite hole, try a different ball ability, and work toward a more
precise route. A local Daily Challenge offers a separate set of holes without
an online leaderboard.

The proposed desktop edition includes the full course in one purchase, with no
in-game purchases or separate game account. This sentence is conditional on
owner approval of the commercial model and final build verification.
